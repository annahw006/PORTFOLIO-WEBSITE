About Me

Turning ideas into living,
breathing digital worlds

I am a multidisciplinary designer and developer based in Nairobi, passionate about creating work that resonates on both an intellectual and emotional level. My process begins with deep listening and careful observation of the world around me, translating insights into design systems that feel inevitable and human. I believe beautiful interfaces are not decoration — they are clarity.

With roots in fine arts and a foundation in software engineering, I bring a rare dual perspective to every project. My work spans brand identity, digital products, and immersive web experiences — always guided by the question: what does this feel like to the person on the other side?

What I Do

Expertise &
disciplines

Every project demands a different mix of skills. Here is the toolkit I have built over eight years of working with studios, startups, and international brands to bring their visions to life.

01

UI / UX Design

From wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes, I design interfaces that balance beauty with function, always centred on the end user's needs and mental models.

02

Brand Identity

Developing coherent visual identities — logos, typography systems, colour palettes — that communicate a brand's essence across every touchpoint and medium.

03

Front-End Dev

Writing clean, performant HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I bridge the gap between design and engineering, turning complex interactions into smooth, accessible code.

04

Motion Design

Animation and micro-interaction design that gives interfaces a sense of life — thoughtful transitions that guide attention without overwhelming the senses.

05

Art Direction

Overseeing the visual tone of editorial, advertising and digital campaigns — selecting photographers, illustrators, and coordinating a unified creative vision.

06

Strategy

Defining design strategy that aligns business goals with user needs. Working with stakeholders to identify opportunities, shape roadmaps, and measure creative impact.
